## Evidence Status

- Learning validation Round 1: not started.
- Participant evidence: not available yet.

## Decision

### Decision route selected

`Pause / rethink`

### Why this route is selected now

Checkpoint 15 requires an evidence-based product decision.
At this moment, no participant learning evidence has been captured in Round 1.
Without learner observations, confidence signals, and confusion patterns, selecting a feature expansion route would be speculative.

## Interpretation

This is a controlled pause, not a project stop.

The product direction remains valid, but progression to feature-oriented phases is gated until learner evidence is collected.

## Required Next Actions

1. Execute the learning validation sessions using [docs/validation/03-learning-validation-session-runbook.md](docs/validation/03-learning-validation-session-runbook.md).
2. Complete [docs/validation/04-learning-validation-results-round-1.md](docs/validation/04-learning-validation-results-round-1.md) with real participant data.
3. Re-run Checkpoint 15 decision once Round 1 evidence is complete.

## Explicitly Deferred

Until Round 1 evidence is available, do not start:

- branching features;
- learner interaction features;
- authoring workflow expansion;
- advanced event-model redesign.

## Re-evaluation Trigger

Re-open this decision immediately after the Round 1 report includes:

- participant counts;
- structured question outcomes;
- scoring trends (if used);
- outcome classification (`STRONG SIGNAL`, `USEFUL BUT PASSIVE`, or `CONFUSING`);
- concrete change recommendations.
